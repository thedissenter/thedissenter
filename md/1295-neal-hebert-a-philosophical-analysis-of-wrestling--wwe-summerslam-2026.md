In this episode, we do a philosophical analysis of WWE SummerSlam 2026. We start with a general overview of SummerSlam, referring to some of the themes explored in Kayfabe Nation. We do a match-by-match analysis of the two nights, and we end with some final thoughts about the event and the current state of WWE.
