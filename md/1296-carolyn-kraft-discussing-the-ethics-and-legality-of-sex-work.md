We discuss what counts as sex work, and the different approaches that people have to it. We explore the emotional and sexual labor surrounding it, and we refer to the variety of experiences that sex workers report. We have a lengthy discussion about the potential negative impact of sex work on society, including how porn can impact romantic relationships. We discuss how we should deal with the fact that people have different perspectives on sex work. We also discuss why modern dating is so messed up, and the factors behind it. Finally, we talk about the different legal approaches to sex work.
