Dr. Emily Towner is a postdoc in cognitive neuroscience at Caltech. Her research focuses on adolescence and the brain. Her interests include loneliness, anxiety, mental health, and social media.

In this episode, we talk about the effects of social isolation on adolescents. We discuss how Dr. Towner studied that in her PhD, and whether virtual interactions can reduce social isolation. We talk about self-disclosure in online environments. We discuss whether the COVID-19 pandemic affected social interaction among emerging adults. We talk about the ongoing discussion surrounding the potential mental health outcomes of social media use. Finally, we talk about Dr. Towner’s postdoc project.
