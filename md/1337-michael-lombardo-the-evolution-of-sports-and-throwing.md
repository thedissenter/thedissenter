Dr. Michael Lombardo is Emeritus Professor of Biology at Grand Valley State University. His research topics include ornithology, animal behavior, climate impacts on birds, and human evolution (such as throwing and projectile weapons).

In this episode, we start by talking about the evolutionary basis of sports. We discuss sex differences in sports interest and motivation. We talk about the evolution of throwing, including primate antecedents to it. Finally, we discuss sex differences in sports ability.
