Dr. Roderick Beaton is Emeritus Koraes Professor of Modern Greek & Byzantine History, Language & Literature at King’s College London. He is the author of Europe: A New History.

In this episode, we focus on Europe: A New History. We talk about the origins of the European identity, what “Europe” really is, why we distinguish between Western Europe and Eastern Europe, and whether the European identity is a strong one. We explore the history of Europe, with a focus on the Roman Empire; European colonization, and the concept of “civilization”; decolonization after the World Wars; and the formation of the European Union. We discuss how we think about the war in Ukraine, and far-right narratives surrounding immigration. Finally, we discuss what the “West” is.
