RECORDED ON NOVEMBER 17th 2026.  
Dr. Joanna Moncrieff is Professor of Critical and Social Psychiatry at University College London, and a practicing psychiatrist at the NHS in London. She has an interest in the history, philosophy and politics of psychiatry, and particularly in the use, misuse and misrepresentation of psychiatric drugs. Her latest book is Chemically Imbalanced: The Making and Unmaking of the Serotonin Myth.

In this episode, we focus on Chemically Imbalanced. We start by talking about how depression used to be approached, and how the chemical imbalance theory came to be. We discuss why the focus was on serotonin. We talk about what depression is, how antidepressants work, whether they actually work, and their negative short-term and long-term effects. Finally, we discuss broader issues with the biological approach to psychiatry, and what someone should do if they are prescribed an antidepressant.
