RECORDED ON NOVEMBER 13th 2026.  
Dr. Richard Wolff is Professor of Economics Emeritus at the University of Massachusetts, Amherst. He is currently a Visiting Professor in the Graduate Program in International Affairs of the New School University, New York City. He is also the co-founder of Democracy at Work and host of their nationally syndicated show Economic Update. His latest book is Question Everything: Professor Richard Wolff on Becoming an American Marxist (Richard D. Wolff in Conversation with Ian J. Seda-Irizarry).

In this episode, we focus on Question Everything. We explore Dr. Wolff’s early influences, and how he first got across Marxism. We talk about what it was like for him to be a Marxist academic, the hostility toward Marxism within the discipline of economics, and the ideology behind mainstream economics. Finally, we talk about why people should be hopeful about the future.
