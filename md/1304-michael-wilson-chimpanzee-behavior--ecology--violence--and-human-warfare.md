Dr. Michael Wilson is a Professor in the Department of Ecology, Evolution and Behavior at the University of Minnesota. He studies nonhuman primates to provide comparative data for testing hypotheses about the evolution of distinctively human traits. He focuses on the behavior and ecology of chimpanzees at Gombe National Park, Tanzania.

In this episode, we start by talking about Dr. Wilson’s work with chimpanzees at Gombe National Park, in Tanzania. We discuss how ecology influences behavior, and chimpanzee personality. We talk about how apes are impacted by human activity. We discuss whether warfare is a cultural invention or an evolved adaptation in humans. Finally, we talk about differences in rates of violence between chimpanzees and bonobos.
