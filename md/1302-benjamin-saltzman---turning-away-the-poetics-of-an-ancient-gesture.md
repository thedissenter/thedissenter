Dr. Benjamin Saltzman is a Professor of English Language and Literature at the University of Chicago. His research interests include poetry and poetics, visual studies, hermeneutics, gesture, theory, language, and environmental humanities. He is the author of Turning Away: The Poetics of an Ancient Gesture.

In this episode, we focus on Turning Away. We start by talking about what turning away is, and its different manifestations, the situations that induce it, and the emotions that go associated with it. We analyze works of art like Timanthes's Sacrifice of Iphigenia, Hitchcock’s Psycho, Augustine's Confessions, and Michelangelo’s Fall and Expulsion of Adam and Eve. Finally, we discuss whether we should condemn the behavior and gestures of turning away.
