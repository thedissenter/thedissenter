RECORDED ON NOVEMBER 3rd 2026.  
Dr. Ben Burgis is Adjunct Professor of Philosophy at Rutgers University, columnist at Jacobin, and host of Give Them An Argument With Ben Burgis (@BenBurgisGTAA). He is the author (together with Mike Beggs and Bhaskar Sunkara) of The Blueprint: How Socialism Can Work in the Real World.  

In this episode, we focus on The Blueprint. We start by defining capitalism and socialism, and we explore the strongest critiques of capitalism. We then talk about what a blueprint for socialism would look like. We address the critique that collective economic control will only stifle innovation. We discuss the former Soviet Union, a planned economy, how markets would work under socialism, and the rational allocation of resources. Finally, we discuss whether socialism has always failed.
